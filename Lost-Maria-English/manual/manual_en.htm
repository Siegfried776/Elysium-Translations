Lost Maria
- The Nameless Flower -

[WARNING]
This game contains grotesque content.
Please take care if that is not for you.


[SCENARIO]

In a world after the fall of civilization,
people hurrying to rebuild had at last begun to win back the lives they once had.
It was then that a snowbound town was struck by ghost sightings.

The Beorb mansion, a grand estate on the edge of town.
Ghosts began to be seen there.

Many exorcists went in to lay the spirits to rest.
Two months have passed since.
The matter is still unresolved.


[CHARACTERS]

†  Noah (protagonist)  †
A man who serves as priest in the snowbound town.
After the Beorb curse claimed lives among the townsfolk, he sets out for the Beorb mansion.
At times he shows a coldness in word and manner that hardly befits a priest.
...They say he is cursed by a demon sword.

†  Rod (protagonist)  †
An elite exorcist sent from the Capital, the heart of this world.
He knows a great deal about exorcism
and is a professional in combat.
He has a wife and child, but lives by his own rules.

†  Gustav (supporting character)  †
An old man who runs a weapon shop in Northern Factorea.
A master of an old school of swordsmanship, and the one who taught Noah the sword.


[STATS]

†  Vitality  †
Goes down each time an enemy hits you.
At 0, it's game over.
Using herbs or resting in a bed restores it to the maximum.

†  Lethality  †
How much harm you do to enemies.
The higher it is, the easier killing becomes.

†  Defense  †
How well you protect yourself.
Reduces the damage enemies deal you.

†  Regen  †
In battle, restores this much Vitality every turn.

†  Status  †
The protagonist's condition.
Any ailment is shown in red.
Using herbs or resting in a bed cures it.

†  Likes  †
The protagonist's favorite food.
Eating the food listed here makes stats more likely to go up.


[ITEMS]

Weapon  -sword-
Raises the protagonist's Lethality.
Noah's Bloodrune is a demon sword whose Lethality rises with every enemy it kills,
up to a maximum of 100.

Shield  -shield-
Raises the protagonist's Defense.

Charm  -accessory-
Chains with special effects.

Gun  -gun-
A weapon for covering fire.
Its high Lethality makes it work as a main weapon too,
but ammo is limited, so it's not one to use freely.

Ammo  -bullet-
Reloads a gun.
It only fits guns of the same type, so watch out.

Herb  -herb-
Restores Vitality, cures ailments, doubles the Time Gauge, and more.
Noah can mix herbs, so make good use of that.

Food  -food-
Raises the protagonist's stats.
Every enemy you defeat leaves some, so it's best to keep at least one inventory slot free.

Bounty  -bounty head-
The heads of people wanted by the Capital.
You get them by searching the bodies,
and Capital soldiers pay well for them.

Iron Key  -key-
Used to open locked doors.
Anyone who has learned Lockpick won't need them.
(It's a key item, so it can't be thrown away. You can still sell it in town.)


[SYSTEM]

†  Zapping  †
In this game you control the protagonists separately.
A NEW GAME starts with Noah; partway through you can control Rod as well.
In battle, if another protagonist is in the same room,
they back you up as a Supporter.
A Supporter can attack with a gun or help with herbs.
In that case, the gold you get at the end of the battle is split between you.

†  Inventory limit  †
Each protagonist can carry up to 7 items.
You'll fill up fast, so throw away what you don't need and eat your food.

†  Resting in the mansion  †
There are a few places in the mansion where you can rest,
but another protagonist has to be in the same room to keep watch.
So it's best for the protagonists to stick together.

†  Mixing  †
Noah can mix herbs.
For example, mixing two Mendgrass (small heal) gives Mend+Mend (medium heal).
It frees up an inventory slot, which helps when you want to carry a lot.

†  Invest  †
Shops have limited stock,
and their regular deliveries can't always keep up.
So the protagonists can put up gold to have extra stock brought in.


[Q&A]

Q. The story isn't moving forward.
A. In this game, just exploring the mansion won't move the story on.
Try going back to town and resting, for example.
If the conditions are met, some event should be waiting for you.

Q. The enemies are too strong to get any further.
A. Your stats rise with the food you take from enemies,
and every enemy, strong or weak, gives the same amount, so if you really can't win,
try raising your stats on the mansion's upper floors.
Going back to town to learn Sword Arts is also a good idea.
...There are also events you can only see by getting a game over,
so sometimes it may be worth dying.

Q. Any tips for winning boss fights?
A. Heal with mixed herbs, and put a protagonist with a gun on support:
that puts the battle in your favor.
