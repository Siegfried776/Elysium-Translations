Lost Maria
- The Nameless Flower -

[WARNING]
This game contains grotesque content.
Please take care if you are sensitive to it.


[SCENARIO]

In a world after the fall of civilization, people rushing to rebuild have at last begun to win back the lives they once had.
Then ghost sightings strike a snowbound town.

On the edge of town stands the grand Beorb mansion.
Some time ago, people began seeing ghosts there.

Many exorcists have gone in to lay the spirits to rest.
Two months on, the matter remains unresolved.


[CHARACTERS]

†  Noah (protagonist)  †
The priest of the snowbound town.
When the Beorb curse claims lives among the townsfolk, he heads for the Beorb mansion.
At times his words and manner are colder than any priest's should be.
Rumor has it he is cursed by a demon sword.

†  Rod (protagonist)  †
An elite exorcist sent from the Capital, the heart of this world.
He knows exorcism inside out and is a seasoned professional in combat.
He has a wife and child, but his motto is to live as he pleases.

†  Gustav (supporting character)  †
An old man who runs the weapon shop in Northern Factorea.
A master of an old school of swordsmanship, he taught Noah the sword.


[STATS]

†  Vitality  †
Drops each time an enemy hits you.
At 0, it's game over.
Herbs or a rest in bed restore it to full.

†  Lethality  †
How much damage you deal to enemies.
The higher it is, the easier they are to kill.

†  Defense  †
How well you protect yourself.
Reduces the damage enemies deal you.

†  Regen  †
In battle, restores this much Vitality each turn.

†  Status  †
The protagonist's condition.
Any ailment shows in red.
Herbs or a rest in bed cure it.

†  Likes  †
The protagonist's favorite food.
Eat it, and your stats are more likely to go up.


[ITEMS]

Weapon  -sword-
Raises Lethality.
Noah's Bloodrune is a demon sword that grows deadlier with every enemy it kills, up to 100 Lethality.

Shield  -shield-
Raises Defense.

Charm  -accessory-
Chains with special effects.

Gun  -gun-
A weapon for covering fire.
It hits hard enough to serve as a main weapon, but ammo is limited, so don't waste it.

Ammo  -bullet-
Reloads a gun.
It only fits guns of the matching type.

Herb  -herb-
Restores Vitality, cures ailments, doubles the Time Gauge and more.
Noah can mix herbs, so put that to good use.

Food  -food-
Raises stats.
Every enemy you defeat drops some, so keep at least one inventory slot free.

Bounty  -bounty head-
The heads of people wanted by the Capital.
Search the bodies to find them, then take them to a Capital soldier for a good price.

Iron Key  -key-
Opens a locked door.
Anyone who has learned Lockpick won't need one.
(It's a key item, so you can't throw it away, but you can sell it in town.)


[SYSTEM]

†  Zapping  †
You control the protagonists separately.
A NEW GAME starts with Noah; later on, you can control Rod too.
In battle, another protagonist in the same room backs you up as a Supporter, with a gun or with herbs.
The gold from that battle is then split between you.

†  Inventory limit  †
Each protagonist can carry up to 7 items.
You'll run out of room fast, so throw away what you don't need and eat your food.

†  Resting in the mansion  †
The mansion has a few places to rest, but another protagonist has to be in the same room to keep watch.
So it pays to keep the protagonists together.

†  Mixing  †
Noah can mix herbs.
For example, two Mendgrass (small heal) make one Mend+Mend (medium heal).
That frees up an inventory slot, which helps when you want to carry more.

†  Invest  †
Shops have limited stock, and their regular deliveries can't always keep up.
You can put up gold to have extra stock brought in.


[Q&A]

Q. The story isn't moving forward.
A. Exploring the mansion alone won't move the story on.
Try going back to town and resting, for example.
If you've met the conditions, an event should be waiting somewhere.

Q. The enemies are too strong to get any further.
A. Your stats rise with the food you take from enemies, and every enemy gives the same amount, strong or weak. If you really can't win, build up your stats on the mansion's upper floors.
Going back to town to learn Sword Arts helps too.
And some events only happen after a game over, so now and then, dying might be worth a try.

Q. Any tips for winning boss fights?
A. Heal with mixed herbs, and give a protagonist with a gun the Supporter role.
That tips the battle in your favor.
